# 1. Profile Optimization

Your profile is your search listing. Instagram search matches the **name field** and **@handle** most heavily, then the bio.

## The handle problem: `beyond_the_formationn`

The double **n** hurts you. People who type "beyond the formation" will get close to your handle but won't match it exactly. They can't remember the extra letter, so they can't tag you.

Fix it with one of these, best first:
1. Check whether `@beyondtheformation`, `@beyond.the.formation` or `@beyondformation` is free. If one is, switch to it. Instagram keeps your followers, but old @-tags stop linking, so switch early.
2. If none is free, keep the handle and make the **name field** do the keyword work (below). Always write the brand as **Beyond The Formation** in captions and on-screen, so search picks up the phrase.

## Name field (max 64 characters, searchable)

Put your main keyword here. It's the only profile text besides the handle that search really weights.

```
Beyond The Formation | Football Tactics
```
Alternatives:
- `Beyond The Formation ⚽ Football Tactics & Analysis`
- `Beyond The Formation | Tactical Breakdowns`

## Bio (150 characters)

Lead with the keywords, then add a reason to follow and a call to action.

```
⚽ Football tactics explained in 60 seconds
📐 Formations • Player roles • Match analysis
🔔 New breakdown every matchday
👇 Request a team/tactic
```

Rules:
- Use keyword phrases people actually search for: *football tactics, formation, tactical analysis, Premier League*.
- Don't fill the bio with hashtags. They're clickable, but they send people away from your page.
- Tag a second account only if you have one, like a YouTube or a newsletter.

## Account settings
- **Professional account → Creator → category "Sports" or "Digital creator"**. Only professional accounts get insights, trial reels and Google indexing.
- **Settings → Account privacy → Public**, and keep "Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results" **ON**. That gets Google to index your posts.
- Add a **link** (Linktree/Beacons or a broadcast channel) with a request form.

## Profile picture
A simple **BTF** monogram or a tactics-board icon that reads clearly at 40 px. Use the same image on every platform.

## Story Highlights (your "menu")
Plain covers, each with a clear label:
`Formations` · `Player Roles` · `Match Analysis` · `Quizzes` · `Requests` · `About`

## Pinned posts (3)
1. Your best-performing Reel, as proof.
2. A "What is Beyond The Formation?" Reel: 15 seconds on what you post and when.
3. An evergreen explainer, e.g. *"4-3-3 vs 4-2-3-1 explained"*.
