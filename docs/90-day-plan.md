# 90-Day Plan

## Days 1–7: Foundation
- [ ] Check whether a cleaner handle is free (`@beyondtheformation`, `@beyond.the.formation`); switch if one is
- [ ] Name field: `Beyond The Formation | Football Tactics`
- [ ] Rewrite the bio (template in `01-profile-optimization.md`)
- [ ] Switch to a Creator account and turn on search-engine indexing
- [ ] Create 6 Highlight covers and 3 pinned posts
- [ ] Build pitch and graphic templates in Canva/CapCut
- [ ] Fill in `data/keywords.json` with the clubs your audience follows
- [ ] Add alt text and keyword first lines to your top 10 older posts (captions can be edited)

## Days 8–30: Consistency
- [ ] Post 5 Reels and 2 carousels every week, following `06-posting-calendar.md`
- [ ] Check every caption with `tools/seo_check.py` (score ≥ 80)
- [ ] Test 2 hooks a week with Trial Reels
- [ ] Reply to every comment within the first hour
- [ ] Start a Broadcast Channel

## Days 31–60: Scale what works
- [ ] Weekly KPI review (`08-analytics-kpis.md`)
- [ ] Launch a named series ("Formation of the Week")
- [ ] 1 collab a week with a similar-sized football page
- [ ] Cross-post to YouTube Shorts and TikTok

## Days 61–90: Authority
- [ ] Big evergreen carousels: "Every formation explained"
- [ ] Monthly "Tactical Awards" post (tags players and clubs, which people share)
- [ ] Pitch collabs with bigger pages using your best numbers
- [ ] Consider a newsletter or YouTube long-form so you're not dependent on one platform

**90-day goal:** non-follower reach above 60%, sends per reach at or above 1%, and profile visits from search growing every week.
