# 7. Growth & Engagement

## First 60 minutes after posting
- Reply to **every** comment with a question, so the thread keeps going.
- Share the post to your Story with a poll or quiz sticker.
- Send it to your **Broadcast Channel**.

## Collabs (fastest way to reach new followers)
- Use **Collab posts** with other football pages of a similar size: both audiences see the post and the numbers are shared.
- Formats: "Tactics vs Stats" (you plus a stats page), "Fan vs Analyst" (you plus a club fan page), "Duel": two analysts, one match.
- Aim for **one collab a week**. Pitch in DMs with a finished script so it's easy to say yes.

## Community
- **Requests:** "Comment a team and I'll break down their system." Then make the video and tag the person who asked. They'll share it.
- **Broadcast Channel** "BTF Tactics Room": early previews, polls, behind the scenes.
- **Series** bring people back: "Tactical Masterclass #1, #2…", "Formation of the Week".
- Pin the best comment on each post.

## Cross-platform
Post every Reel to **YouTube Shorts** and **TikTok** too, without watermarks. The same keyword captions work there, and YouTube Shorts rank on Google.

## Don'ts
- Buying followers or using engagement pods. Fake accounts lower your engagement rate and reach.
- Reposting TV footage without changing it. It gets flagged, muted or removed.
- Deleting and reposting weak posts. Archive them instead.
