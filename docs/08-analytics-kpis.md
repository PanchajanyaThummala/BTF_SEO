# 8. Analytics & KPIs

Check **Insights** weekly. Compare **rates per reach**, not raw numbers.

| KPI | Formula | Target | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sends / reach** | shares ÷ reach | ≥ 1% | The strongest signal for distributing Reels |
| **Saves / reach** | saves ÷ reach | ≥ 1.5% (explainers, carousels) | Content worth coming back to |
| **Avg. watch %** | avg watch time ÷ length | ≥ 60% | Hook and pacing quality |
| **3-second hold** | viewers past 3 s ÷ plays | ≥ 70% | Hook strength |
| **Non-follower reach** | % of reach from non-followers | ≥ 60% on Reels | Discovery via Explore and Reels |
| **Follows / reach** | follows ÷ reach | ≥ 0.3% | Did the Reel turn viewers into followers? |
| **Profile visits from search** | Insights → Profile activity | Rising week on week | Whether the SEO is working |

## Weekly review (20 min)
1. Put the week's posts in a sheet: pillar, hook, length, the 5 KPIs.
2. Find the **top 2** posts and the **bottom 2**.
3. Top: make a follow-up or part 2 within 7 days.
4. Bottom: was it the hook, the topic or the timing? Change one thing.
5. Move time towards the pillar with the best **sends per reach**.
