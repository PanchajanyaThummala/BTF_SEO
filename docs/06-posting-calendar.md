# 6. Posting Calendar

**Target:** 5 Reels + 2 carousels a week, and Stories every day. Keep to it. Posting regularly matters more than posting a lot.

| Day | Main post | Pillar | Stories |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Reel: weekend's biggest tactical story | Breakdown | Poll: "Best tactical performance of the weekend?" |
| **Tue** | Carousel: "5 tactical takeaways from matchweek X" | Breakdown | Answer the poll + requests box |
| **Wed** | Reel: midweek UCL/UEL match analysis (post-match) | Breakdown | Live match reactions |
| **Thu** | Reel: Formation / concept explained | Explained | Quiz sticker |
| **Fri** | Reel: Weekend preview, "How X will set up vs Y" | Player role / preview | Predictions slider |
| **Sat** | Reel: Hot take or quiz | Debate / Interactive | Live reactions, share fans' DMs |
| **Sun** | Carousel or Reel: Big-match breakdown | Breakdown | Week recap, tease next week |

## Posting times
Start with **18:00–21:00 local time for your largest audience** (check Insights → Audience → Most active times). After a big match, speed matters more than the clock: post within 2–6 hours.

## Batch workflow
- **Sunday night:** script the Thu/Fri Reels (evergreen and preview).
- **Match days:** record screenshots and notes live, then edit with pre-made pitch templates.
- Keep a **backlog of 5 evergreen explainers** for international breaks.

## International breaks and off-season
Post evergreen explainers, "greatest tactical teams ever" series (Sacchi's Milan, Guardiola's Barça, Klopp's Liverpool), transfer-fit analysis ("How {new signing} fits {Team}'s system") and "build your XI" interactives.
