# 4. Reels Playbook

Reels are where non-followers find you. Four signals matter most: **sends per reach, watch time, likes per reach, rewatches**.

## Structure (30–60 s performs best for analysis)

| Time | Beat | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Hook**, on-screen and spoken | *"Arsenal scored this goal before Chelsea even touched the ball."* |
| 2–8 s | **Setup**: the problem | *"Chelsea pressed with 3 players. Here's the trick."* |
| 8–45 s | **Breakdown**: 2–3 clear points, arrows, freeze frames | |
| 45–55 s | **Payoff**: the insight | *"That's why Arteta's full-back tucks inside."* |
| last 3 s | **Loop / CTA** | *"Send this to the mate who says Arsenal are boring."* |

## Hook formulas
- **Curiosity:** "Nobody is talking about what [Manager] changed at half-time."
- **Bold claim:** "[Player] is the most underrated [role] in Europe. Here's the proof."
- **Question:** "Why does every top team play with an inverted full-back now?"
- **Negative:** "The mistake that cost [Team] the game."
- **Number:** "3 tactical reasons [Team] won the derby."

## Production checklist
- [ ] Hook text in the first frame, big and inside the safe zone (away from the top and bottom 15%)
- [ ] Keyword spoken within 5 seconds
- [ ] Subtitles on (most people watch muted)
- [ ] A cut or visual change every 2–3 s
- [ ] 9:16, 1080×1920, no watermarks from other apps
- [ ] Cover image with a clear title (it shows in the profile grid)
- [ ] Trending or original audio at low volume under the voiceover
- [ ] Alt text and a keyword caption added

## Trial Reels
Use **Trial Reels** (shown to non-followers first) to test 2 hooks for the same video. Keep the hook that wins on watch time and sends.

## Timing
Post **within 2–6 hours after a big match ends**. Search demand for "[Team A] vs [Team B]" is at its peak then. Prepare the pitch templates before kick-off so you only need to add the moments afterwards.
