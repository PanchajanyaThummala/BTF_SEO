# 3. Content Pillars

Five pillars. Each one is original analysis, which Instagram rewards over reposted clips.

| # | Pillar | Share of posts | Why it ranks | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tactical Breakdown** | 35% | High watch time, saves | *"Why Man City's press failed vs Arsenal"* |
| 2 | **Formation / Concept Explained** | 20% | Evergreen search traffic | *"What is an inverted full-back? Explained in 45s"* |
| 3 | **Player Role Profile** | 15% | Player-name searches, fan shares | *"Why Rodri is the most important player in world football"* |
| 4 | **Debate / Hot Take** | 15% | DM sends and comments | *"This 4-2-3-1 is the best of the decade. Change my mind"* |
| 5 | **Interactive** (quiz, "build your XI", guess the team from its formation) | 15% | Comments, sends, repeat viewers | *"Guess the team from the average positions 👀"* |

## Carousel formats (still great for saves and Google)
- "5 tactical trends from matchweek X" (one slide per trend)
- "Formation cheat sheet: 4-3-3 vs 3-5-2" (people save these)
- Stats-plus-pitch-map slides after big games

## Copyright-safe visuals
Match footage gets flagged and suppressed. Use instead:
- **Tactics-board animations** (Tactical Pad, Tactica, or Canva/CapCut with pitch templates)
- Screenshots with **heavy annotation** plus your voiceover, which makes them transformative
- Stats graphics built from public data such as FBref
- Face-cam or voiceover explainers

Being original keeps you out of "unoriginal content" suppression, and your own voice is what makes the page a brand.
