## Pre-recording rules (so the Reels don't go stale)

1. **Never say "this week", "tomorrow" or "in 5 days".** Say **"on 26 October"**.
2. **Keep odds out of your voice.** Put them on a graphic you update when you edit, because odds move.
3. **Put stats on screen as well as in your voice**, so a corrected number only needs a text change.
4. **Leaks happen.** In 2024 news of the result leaked before the ceremony. If a credible leak appears, post the leak reaction (B8) and stop the "who will win" Reels.
5. **Film B8 in two versions now** (Kane wins / Yamal wins) and add a 5-second opener on the night. You'll post within minutes while bigger pages are still editing.
