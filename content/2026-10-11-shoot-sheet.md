# Shoot Sheet: Sunday 11 Oct (one session, 5 Reels)

**Minimum session (30 min):** Reels 1 + 2, which go out **today**.
**Full session (~90 min):** all 5, which covers you through Wednesday.

---

## Before you hit record (5 min)
- [ ] Phone **vertical**, 1080p or 4K, **30fps**, at eye level, lens wiped
- [ ] Face a window (soft light on your face), nothing bright behind you
- [ ] Mic clipped on, or phone within an arm's length
- [ ] Teleprompter app with this file open, scrolling slowly
- [ ] **Two tops**: one for topical Reels (1–3), one for the Ballon d'Or series (4–5). Posting different days in the same shirt looks batch-filmed; the series shirt makes 4–5 look like a set.

## How to record each Reel
1. **Clap once** at the start of each take (makes syncing and cutting easy in the edit)
2. Record the **hook 3 times** with different energy (calm / punchy / deadpan). You'll pick the best one in the edit, or test two with Trial Reels.
3. Record the **body in one or two takes**. Stumbles are fine; cut them out later.
4. `[PAUSE]` = a half-second pause before the punchline. That's where the sarcasm lands.

## 🎞️ Reaction library (record once, use for weeks: 3 min)
Ten 2-second clips, no talking, for sarcastic cutaways in any Reel:
eye-roll · slow head-shake · facepalm · "are you serious?" stare · slow clap · laugh · shrug · pointing at the screen · fake shocked gasp · "I told you" smirk
