## 4. Matchday commentary kit

### Stay copyright-safe (this matters)
- **Never film the TV or stream, and never use broadcast audio.** Premier League footage gets muted, removed or struck, and a strike limits reach on your whole account.
- Use **your face, your voice, a tactics board, lineup graphics and stat graphics**. Avoid press-agency photos (Getty, etc.).
- Your edge is the **explanation**, not the footage. Big pages post the clip; you explain what it meant.

### Three commentary formats
1. **Kick-off Story (10 s):** lineup graphic + one sentence: "Watch Liverpool's front three on City's goalkeeper. That's the game." Add a poll sticker.
2. **Half-time take (30 s, face-cam):** "Three things from the first half: 1… 2… 3…" Post as a Story straight away. Keep the best one for the post-match Reel.
3. **Instagram Live watch-along (optional):** you + the board, no screen in shot. Title it with keywords: *"Liverpool vs Man City LIVE tactical watch-along"*. Pin a question and reply to comments by name.

### What to watch in each game (so your takes are sharp)
| Match | Watch for |
|---|---|
| **Arsenal v Leeds** | Does Leeds' unbeaten side sit deep or press? How does Arsenal create against a low block: overloads on one wing, cut-backs, set pieces? |
| **Man United v Spurs** | Carrick's 4-2-3-1 vs De Zerbi's build-up from the goalkeeper. Does United press it or sit off? United shoot a lot (most shots in the league) but concede in transition (1 clean sheet in 7). Spurs have scored 2 in 5. |
| **Liverpool v Man City** | Iraola's press (the league's most intense) vs City's build-up (best passing under pressure). Who wins the first 15 minutes? Where is Elliot Anderson receiving? Who leads Liverpool's attack if Isak and Gakpo are out? |
| **Leeds v Man United** (MW7) | Leeds' structure at home vs United's front four. Who controls midfield? |
| **Forest v Arsenal** (MW7) | Glasner's back three and wing-backs vs Arsenal's wingers. Who wins the wide 2v1s? |

### Commentary lines that drive sends and comments
- "Send this to the [club] fan who said [player] can't play in a press."
- "Tell me I'm wrong 👇"
- "Pause on this frame: where's the free man?" (gets rewatches)
