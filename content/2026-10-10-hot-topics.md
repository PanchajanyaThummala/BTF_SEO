## ⚠️ Rules for controversy content (read first)

1. **State facts with attribution.** Say "an independent commission found City guilty", never "City are cheats". Always add: **"City deny wrongdoing and are appealing."**
2. **Label opinions as opinions:** "Hot take:", "I think…", "Change my mind".
3. **No rumours as facts.** Reports of dressing-room fights or sackings come from single outlets. Say "reports claim…" or leave them out.
4. **Argue about football, not people.** No insults about players' character, families or nationality. Instagram limits reach on content flagged as harassment.
5. **Moderate comments.** Turn on Settings → Hidden Words → hide offensive comments. Pin a sharp comment from each side.
6. **No match footage.** Use the tactics board, graphics, your face and screenshots of headlines with the source named.
